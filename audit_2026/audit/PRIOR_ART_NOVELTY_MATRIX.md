## D. LWIR physics (does fog/rain degrade thermal like visible?)

- Munis & Delaney (CRREL 1975): at moderate droplet concentration, extinction at 10.6 µm ≈ extinction at 0.63 µm; at high concentration visible extinction keeps rising while 10.6 µm plateaus.
- Duthon, Colomb, Bernardin (*Appl. Sci.* 2019, DOI 10.3390/app9142843): fog extinction nearly wavelength-independent through VIS–NIR and depends on droplet-size distribution (radiation vs advection fog); no LWIR data.
- Perić et al. (*Sensors* 2019, DOI 10.3390/s19153313): Beer–Lambert range model σ_sc = 3.91 (λ/0.55)^−q, q = 0.585 V^(1/3); LWIR scatters far less than visible at visibility > ~1 km but water-vapour absorption makes MWIR superior in humid conditions.
- Judd et al. (SPIE 2019, DOI 10.1117/12.2519423; abstract UNVERIFIED): LWIR reportedly less affected than SWIR/VIS/lidar only in light-to-moderate fog.
- Krišto et al. (2020): fog "disperses LWIR radiation" and cuts detection range in real data.
- Deirmendjian (*J. Appl. Meteorol.* 1975): rain as volumetric Mie attenuation 12 µm–2 cm.

**Consequences.** (i) The dehazing model I = J·t + A(1−t) with a sky airlight term is defensible for LWIR only as a *contrast-loss toward ambient path radiance* model; adaptive gamma is a contrast-stretch heuristic, not a physical inverse. (ii) **No peer-reviewed study of falling-rain streak formation in LWIR imagery was found**; the only measured droplet effect is windshield droplets reducing captured radiance (local attenuation/blur). An LSRB "rain-streak removal" CNN trained on visible-style additive streaks has no physical justification for thermal input, and the zer0-data `docs/theory.md` additive rain model is imported from visible-light work without evidence.

## E. Answers to the brief's questions

1. **Is the original GATE-IR architecture novel?** No. Every component has a verified precedent: translator stage (A1, 2019, public code), P2 head on YOLOv8 for FLIR v2 (A10, 2024), ViT component in YOLOv8 on FLIR v2 (A11, 2025), frozen RGB teacher + GAN-synthesised visible input + multi-level feature mimic + thermal-only inference (A2, Nov 2025), weather-classifier → conditional classical preprocessing → YOLOv8 (B3, June 2026; B4, B5).
2. **Is CycleGAN + YOLO + feature mimic routine?** Yes. Plain MSE feature mimic is the *baseline* in FreqKD (A4) and InfraNet (A9); CGDet (A2) is the near-identical pipeline.
3. **Would hard gating, soft gating, reliability-weighted KD, or risk-aware enhancement rename existing mechanisms?** Hard gating = B3/B4; soft gating = B2 (GDIP); reliability-weighted KD = A9/B16; risk-aware cost-aware action selection = B6 + B10/B11 + B12 (the objective is learning-to-defer / selective prediction). The specific *combination* (label-free per-image detection-loss regressor + explicit latency cost + classical thermal enhancers + identity) is not found in one paper, but every piece is published and the 2ε regret bound is elementary.
4. **The one narrow contribution that might survive a competent 2026 reviewer** is not a method. It is a controlled, released, multi-seed study on a fixed 3-class FLIR ADAS v2 protocol that isolates questions nobody has answered: (a) **does an unpaired CycleGAN pseudo-RGB teacher transfer anything beyond what a no-teacher student or a same-modality thermal teacher gives?** (teacher-input ablation: no teacher / thermal-trained YOLOv8-L teacher / COCO YOLOv8-L on pseudo-RGB / COCO YOLOv8-L on replicated-grayscale thermal), and (b) **does uniform enhancement hurt already-detectable thermal targets, so that an oracle per-image choice has headroom at all?** (B4/B5 of the experiment matrix). If (b) shows no oracle headroom, the entire routing programme is dead on arrival and that negative result is itself publishable in an applied venue.
5. **If no novelty remains:** see `PUBLICATION_READINESS_DECISION.md` for the ranked alternatives (reproducibility/ablation paper; real-weather thermal evaluation using UNIRI-TID/ZUT-FIR-ADAS/DENSE with oracle-headroom analysis; or stop).
